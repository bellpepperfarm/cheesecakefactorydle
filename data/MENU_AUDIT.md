# Menu audit — 11 October 2026

Source: [The Cheesecake Factory Summer 2026 nutritional guide](https://www.thecheesecakefactory.com/nutrition). The raw `menu.json` import is unchanged.

378 positive-calorie entries with photos were reviewed. The new playable snapshot contains 224 entries; 154 imported entries are held out. Duplicate listings in different menu categories are listed separately below.

This is a conservative eligibility review, not a claim that every excluded dish is fictitious or incorrect. Unmatched names and changed recipe values are held pending manual confirmation. The original snapshot is retained only for migration/recovery; it is not available for play. Public archives begin on 2026-10-11. The live Daily and sorting pools use the reviewed version. Future imports apply `data/policies/cheesecake-factory.json`, including a review gate for new or changed entries.

The migration keeps the original shuffled ID order and skips excluded IDs. Clean existing daily selections remain the same; historical or previously saved games are not relabeled as newly verified data.

## Scope and limits

- All eligible local image paths were matched against the repository files; images must be committed to ship.
- The guide was used for exact dish/portion and calorie cross-checks. Missing exact matches are not evidence that a dish is invalid.
- Optional-side combinations, calorie ranges, customizable dishes and near-zero drinks were removed from the active pool.
- Appetizer guesses are explicitly for the full order, not calories per person. Lunch and regular portions are labeled separately.
- All 202 unique photos in the initial candidate pool were decoded and visually checked in contact sheets. Egg/omelette and SkinnyLicious burger/sandwich plates with ambiguous accompanying sides were then removed. This is a sanity check, not a measurement of portion sizes from photographs.
- The extremely high hot chocolate value (820) is present in the official guide; it is retained rather than removed merely for being surprising.
